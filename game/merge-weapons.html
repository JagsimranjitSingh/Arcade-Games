Game Overview

Merge Weapons combines the satisfying merge mechanic of combination puzzles with an auto-battler combat system. Your job is to buy basic weapons from the shop, drag identical weapons together to merge them into more powerful versions, and equip your heroes with the strongest gear to fight increasingly difficult waves of enemies. It's a game of economic efficiency, merge optimization, and strategic hero loadout management.

The merge system follows a straightforward progression: two identical Level 1 swords merge into a Level 2 sword, two Level 2s become Level 3, and so on up to the legendary Level 10 weapons. Each level dramatically increases the weapon's damage output, attack speed, and special ability potency. The exponential power curve means a single Level 8 weapon outperforms dozens of Level 1 weapons, making merge efficiency the core skill that determines your progression speed.

The combat system runs automatically — your equipped heroes attack enemy waves based on their weapon stats and positioning. You don't control the combat directly; instead, your strategic influence comes from which weapons you equip, which heroes you assign them to, and how you arrange your hero formation. Tank heroes benefit from slow, high-damage weapons that stagger enemies. Ranged heroes excel with fast-firing weapons. Support heroes amplify their buff effects with special ability-focused weapon types.

The economy loop is carefully balanced. Defeating enemy waves earns gold for purchasing new base weapons. Higher-level waves drop more gold but require stronger weapons to clear. Spending too much on base weapons without merging leads to a cluttered board with weak gear. Spending too little and saving gold leads to underpowered heroes who can't clear waves. The sweet spot is a continuous cycle of buy, merge, equip, fight, reinvest. The game features an idle component — your heroes continue fighting while you're away, and you return to accumulated rewards ready for a merge session.

Gameplay Strategy & Pro Tips

Board Management

Your merge board has limited slots. Never let it fill completely — always keep at least 2 empty slots for incoming purchases and merge results. A full board means you can't buy new weapons, breaking the buy-merge cycle. If your board gets crowded, merge lower-level duplicates even if it doesn't produce an immediate power upgrade; clearing space is more valuable than holding every piece.

Gold Efficiency

Buy weapons in pairs to ensure every purchase can be immediately merged. Buying single weapons wastes board space and delays the merge chain. If you can afford 3 identical base weapons, buy all 3 — merge two into a Level 2, save the third for when you can afford the next pair to continue the merge chain upward.

Hero-Weapon Matching

Don't just equip your highest-level weapon on any hero. Match weapon types to hero classes: tanks need slow, heavy weapons for stagger effects; rangers need fast weapons for sustained DPS; supports need special ability weapons for buff amplification. A properly matched Level 6 weapon often outperforms a mismatched Level 7 in actual combat performance.

Wave Progression Strategy

If you can't clear a wave, don't repeatedly attempt it. Instead, replay earlier waves to farm gold, invest in merges, and return with significantly stronger equipment. The gold-per-minute from comfortably farming a lower wave often exceeds the gold-per-minute from struggling against a higher wave. Patience and preparation trump brute-force attempts.

Frequently Asked Questions About Merge Weapons

How does the weapon merging system work?

Drag two identical-level weapons together to create one weapon of the next level. Two Level 1 swords become a Level 2 sword, and so on up to Level 10. Each merge level dramatically increases damage, attack speed, and special ability power. The exponential power scaling makes achieving high-level merges far more effective than accumulating many low-level weapons.

Do my heroes fight automatically?

Yes — combat is fully automated. Your heroes attack enemy waves based on their equipped weapon stats, hero class abilities, and formation positioning. Your strategic input comes from weapon selection, hero-weapon matching, and formation arrangement. This lets you focus on the merge economy while combat runs independently.

Does the game earn progress while I'm offline?

Yes — your heroes continue fighting enemy waves while you're away. When you return, accumulated gold and rewards are waiting for you to invest in new weapons and merges. This idle component means you make progress both during active merge sessions and passive offline periods.

What's the difference between hero classes?

Three hero classes benefit from different weapon types: Tanks excel with slow, heavy weapons that stagger enemies and protect the front line. Rangers deal sustained damage with fast-firing weapons from the back row. Supports amplify team buffs with special ability-focused weapons. Matching weapon type to hero class is essential for optimal combat performance.
