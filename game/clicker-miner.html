Game Overview

Clicker Miner is an incremental idle game where you tap to mine digital currency from an ever-deepening procedural mineshaft, then invest your earnings in automated mining bots that work while you're away. The satisfying click-to-earn core loop is layered with strategic upgrade decisions, prestige mechanics, and a discovery system that reveals rare minerals and hidden chambers as you dig deeper.

Your mining journey begins at the surface with a basic pickaxe. Each tap extracts a small amount of digital currency from the rock face. As you accumulate wealth, you purchase your first automated mining bot — a small drill that generates passive income without tapping. From there, the upgrade tree expands dramatically: bigger drills, laser miners, plasma excavators, quantum tunnelers, and reality-bending cosmic extractors, each generating orders of magnitude more currency than the previous tier.

The depth system adds exploration to the idle formula. Every 100 meters you dig (through accumulated mining output), you reach a new mineral stratum with different visual themes and earning multipliers. Shallow strata contain common minerals, but deeper layers yield rare crystals, ancient fossils, and eventually exotic materials with massive value multipliers. Each stratum also has a chance to contain hidden chambers — bonus rooms with significant one-time rewards and lore fragments that piece together a story about the world beneath the surface.

The prestige system ("Rebirth") allows you to restart your mine with permanent multiplier gems that accelerate your next run. Each Rebirth retains your discovered strata knowledge, making subsequent runs faster to reach deeper layers. The meta-game of optimizing Rebirth timing — balancing current earning rate against the permanent multiplier gain — creates an engaging strategic layer beyond the satisfying core clicking loop. Clicker Miner supports both active tapping sessions (where auto-clickers and manual taps combine for maximum output) and passive idle play (where automated bots handle everything between sessions).

Gameplay Strategy & Pro Tips

First Automation Priority

Your first mining bot purchase is the most important decision. Resist the temptation to buy the cheapest bot immediately — save for the second-tier drill, which has a dramatically better cost-to-income ratio. The time spent tapping to save for a better bot pays off exponentially within the first 5 minutes of automated income.

Active vs. Passive Play Balance

During active play, focus your taps on the highest-value mineral deposits visible in the rock face. Rare deposits flash with a distinctive glow — tapping these yields 5-10x normal currency. Meanwhile, let your automated bots handle the constant base-rate mining. This combination of targeted manual mining and broad automated extraction maximizes active-session income.

Depth Exploration Strategy

Don't rush to the deepest stratum immediately. Each mineral layer has a "saturation point" where your mining bots have extracted the easy resources. Once earning rate plateaus at a given depth, invest in digging deeper to reach higher-value minerals. Think of depth progression as unlocking new earning tiers rather than just reaching a number.

Optimal Rebirth Timing

The ideal Rebirth moment is when your earning rate growth has slowed to under 10% per hour and the Rebirth multiplier gem would at least double your early-game income rate. Rebirthing too early wastes accumulated progress; rebirthing too late means you're earning inefficiently when a multiplier could be accelerating fresh progress. Check your Rebirth bonus preview frequently to identify the crossover point.

Frequently Asked Questions About Clicker Miner

Does Clicker Miner earn resources while I'm offline?

Yes — your automated mining bots continue working while you're away. When you return, all accumulated currency is waiting for you to invest in upgrades. The game reports how much you earned offline and shows any notable discoveries (rare minerals, hidden chambers) your bots uncovered during your absence.

What is the Rebirth/prestige system?

Rebirth allows you to restart your mine from the surface, but you keep permanent multiplier gems that accelerate your next run. Each Rebirth also retains knowledge of discovered strata, making subsequent runs faster to reach deep layers. Timing your Rebirths optimally — when the multiplier gain outweighs continued mining — is a key strategic decision.

What are hidden chambers and how do I find them?

Hidden chambers are bonus rooms discovered randomly at deeper mining depths. They contain significant one-time currency rewards and lore fragments that reveal the story behind the world beneath the surface. Chambers are found through mining depth milestones — the deeper you dig, the more likely you are to discover them.

Should I focus on manual tapping or automated bots?

Both contribute to your mining output. Automated bots provide consistent passive income 24/7, while manual tapping lets you target high-value mineral deposits for burst income during active sessions. The optimal strategy combines both: let bots handle base-rate mining while you manually tap rare deposits that flash with distinctive glows for 5-10x bonus currency.
