Game Overview

Space Scavenger drops you into the cockpit of a salvage ship navigating through a dense asteroid field in deep space. Your mission: collect scattered scrap metal, salvaged technology, and rare crystals to upgrade your ship's shields, engines, and weapons in real-time as threats escalate around you. It's a survival game where resource management meets twitch piloting, demanding both strategic thinking and precise control.

The asteroid field is procedurally generated with increasing density, ensuring each run presents unique navigation challenges. Early zones feature sparse, slow-moving asteroids with generous gaps. As you venture deeper, the field thickens — asteroids spin faster, cluster into formations, and occasionally fragment into smaller, harder-to-avoid shrapnel clouds on impact. Environmental hazards like solar flares (temporary visibility reduction), gravitational wells (pull your ship off course), and debris storms (rapid-fire micro-asteroid bursts) add variety and force you to adapt your piloting style moment-to-moment.

The upgrade system is the strategic backbone of Space Scavenger. Collected scrap feeds three upgrade paths: Shields (absorb more hits before destruction), Engines (increase maneuverability and top speed), and Weapons (deploy a forward-facing laser to break apart asteroids in your path). Each upgrade has five tiers, and the optimal build order depends on your playstyle. Defensive players prioritize shields, speedrunners focus on engines, and aggressive players invest in weapons to carve paths through dense asteroid clusters. Rare crystals act as a premium currency for special one-time-use abilities like a temporary force field or an emergency warp that teleports you to a safe zone.

The game tracks three metrics: maximum distance survived, total scrap collected, and highest upgrade tier achieved. These create three distinct metagames within a single run — do you play it safe to maximize distance, take risks to chase high-value scrap, or hyper-focus upgrades for maximum combat power?

Gameplay Strategy & Pro Tips

Early Zone Resource Priority

In the first 60 seconds, prioritize engine upgrades over shields. Early asteroids are sparse enough that a more maneuverable ship avoids damage entirely, and the increased speed helps you reach more scrap faster. Shift to shield upgrades once asteroid density increases past the point where pure piloting can avoid all hits.

Scrap Collection Routes

Scrap spawns in loose clusters. Rather than chasing individual pieces, identify the cluster center and pilot a sweeping arc through it. Zigzag collection attempts waste time and expose you to collision risk. A smooth, curved approach captures 80% of a cluster's value with a fraction of the danger.

Environmental Hazard Responses

Solar flares: slow down immediately and focus on the narrow illuminated zone directly ahead. Gravitational wells: steer into the pull at a tangent angle to slingshot around rather than fighting against it. Debris storms: activate your force field if available, or retreat to the edge of the storm where micro-asteroid density is lowest.

Weapon Usage Tactics

The forward laser is most effective against large, slow-moving asteroids directly in your path. Don't waste energy shooting fast-moving debris — it's more efficient to dodge. Save weapon upgrades for the deep zones (past 300m distance) where asteroid density makes pure avoidance impossible and controlled path-clearing becomes essential for survival.

Frequently Asked Questions About Space Scavenger

What are the three upgrade paths and which should I prioritize?

Space Scavenger offers Shields (absorb more hits), Engines (better maneuverability and speed), and Weapons (forward laser to break asteroids). The optimal priority depends on your playstyle: engines first for agility-focused survival, shields for a forgiving approach, or weapons for aggressive path-clearing in dense zones.

What are the environmental hazards besides asteroids?

Beyond asteroids, you'll encounter solar flares (reduce visibility temporarily), gravitational wells (pull your ship off course), and debris storms (rapid micro-asteroid bursts). Each hazard requires a different piloting response — slowing down, slingshot maneuvers, or using defensive abilities respectively.

What are rare crystals used for?

Rare crystals are a premium currency for one-time-use special abilities: a temporary force field (absorbs all damage for 5 seconds), an emergency warp (teleports to a safe zone), and a scrap magnet (attracts all nearby scrap for 10 seconds). Crystals are scarce, so choosing when to spend them is a critical strategic decision.

Is there an end goal or is it purely about surviving as long as possible?

Space Scavenger tracks three distinct metrics: maximum distance survived, total scrap collected per run, and highest upgrade tier achieved. This creates three different playstyle goals within the same game. There is no fixed ending — the challenge is pushing your personal records across all three categories.
